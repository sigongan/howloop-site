# Content roadmap

Six guides are live. AdSense approval is much smoother at **15–20**, so this is
the next fourteen, ordered by how well they suit an animation.

The filter for every topic on this list is the same three questions:

1. Does it happen to a lot of people, more than once?
2. Is the confusing part **hidden** — inside a pipe, a case, a menu?
3. Can a careful non-expert do it with ordinary tools?

If the answer to (2) is no, a photo would do the job and the guide is not a
Howloop guide.

## Sequencing suggestion

**Weeks 1–2 — five easy wins.** Bleed a radiator, reset a breaker, squeaking
hinge, descale a kettle, charging port. All quick to draw, all high search
volume, all zero risk.

**Weeks 3–4 — the medium ones.** Dripping tap, washing machine filter, laptop
overheating, draughts. These are the guides people actually search for at
volume; get them right rather than fast.

**Then apply to AdSense** with 15+ guides, a submitted sitemap, and some organic
traffic arriving.

**After approval — build the clusters.** Once a topic works, go deep rather than
wide. "Dripping tap" naturally leads to washer types, cartridge types, and
shutting off the water — internal links between them are what actually moves
rankings.

## Two things to protect

**Don't drift into YMYL.** Medical, financial and legal topics are held to a much
higher standard by both Google and AdSense reviewers. The stuck-ring guide is
about as close to the line as this site should go, and it carries an explicit
"go to a clinic" box at the top for exactly that reason.

**Don't publish anything about bypassing protection.** No defeating safety
interlocks, no removing watermarks you don't own, no circumventing DRM or device
locks. It is an easy category to wander into with how-to content and it is a
fast route to losing an AdSense account.
