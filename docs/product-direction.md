# Howloop product direction

Priority: useful free website, then entertaining animated finance Shorts that bring people to a relevant lesson and calculator. Apple apps and actual transfers or investment execution are later phases.

## Website first
One visitor problem per screen. Put input and the useful result together. Explain each idea with one sentence, a labelled example and one optional knowledge check. Keep detailed education, assumptions and primary sources accessible below. No signup needed for core tools.

Use stable lesson slugs, versioned source checks and calculation modules independent of UI. A Shorts episode should link to one matching lesson/tool, and later appear on that lesson only when a real video exists. No fake channel links or empty player placeholders.

## Traffic and learning
Create a short story around an everyday Australian money problem. Show one hypothetical example; distinguish policy announcements from enacted rules. End with one practical action on Howloop. Measure visits to tools, completed calculations and repeat use before expanding features. Ads must not interrupt the calculation workflow.

## Later: Apple app and money execution
The long-term request includes real money transfers and investment execution. Apple developer membership is an app distribution prerequisite, not a financial-services authorisation. Scope the relevant licences, authorisations and regulated partners before implementation; ASIC approval is not assumed. Start with consent, user-defined amounts and limits, clear confirmation, pause/revoke controls, execution status, duplicate prevention and reconciliation. Treat reading account information, initiating transfers and executing investments as separate capabilities requiring separate review. Do not put bank credentials or private financial data into this educational site.

## Content maintenance
Read the source before changing sourcesChecked/checkedAt. Registry: src/data/policy-updates.json. Lessons: src/data/learning.json. Review warnings use the actual Brisbane date and do not change source dates during builds. Human-readable change notes must explain applicability, including transitions, without converting a political announcement into current law.
