How to convert an overseas driver licence to an Australian one

The rules for exchanging an overseas driver licence are set by each state, not the country, and they are not the same in NSW, Victoria and Queensland. See all three side by side, plus the different path for a recognised-country licence versus one that is not, and find the route that is yours.

Researched, illustrated and safety-checked by the Howloop editorial team · Published 12 August 2026 · Last reviewed 12 August 2026 · How we make guides

! This is a process guide, not legal advice

This page walks through how to convert an overseas driver licence and which route fits your state — it is not advice on immigration status, visa conditions or anything about registering or insuring a vehicle. Road rules and licensing schemes change. Howloop is not a driving instructor or a licensing authority. For your exact situation, use Transport for NSW / Service NSW, VicRoads or the Queensland Department of Transport and Main Roads directly.

Three states, three different schemes, and a different list of “recognised” countries in each one. Pick where you live and the rest fades out — or read all three and compare.

Who are you?

New South Wales Service NSW centre, in person
NSW residents

What you need

  • Your original overseas licence Not a photocopy. If it is lost, an authorisation letter from the issuing country instead.
  • Proof of identity Standard NSW identity documents, and proof of permanent residency if that is your route.
  • An approved translation, if it is not in English Transport for NSW only accepts translations from Multicultural NSW, the Department of Home Affairs, or — for Korean and Taiwanese licences only — the relevant consulate.

What you do

Recognised country

  • Check the current list of recognised countries and jurisdictions on the NSW driving website — it includes places like the UK, USA, Canada, Ireland, Singapore and Japan
  • Take your form, original licence and ID to a service centre
  • No knowledge or driving test if your licence is current, or expired within the last 5 years

Not on the recognised list

  • Book and pass the driver knowledge test
  • Book and pass the driving test
  • Then complete the transfer at a service centre with the same documents

Citizens, permanent residents and NZ licence holders must transfer within 3 months of arriving. Other temporary visa holders can drive on their overseas licence for 6 months before they need to apply for a temporary NSW licence instead. Drivers 75 or older need a medical review as well.

How long

Often the same visit If you are exempt from testing. Otherwise, add the time to book and sit both tests first.

What arrives

A NSW driver licence A receipt lets you keep driving for up to 2 months while the card is posted out.
Victoria VicRoads, in person
Victorian residents

What you need

  • Your current overseas licence or learner permit Valid, and in English or with a translation.
  • Category A and Category B identity documents VicRoads’ standard proof-of-identity combination.
  • Proof of your Victorian address A bank statement, utility bill or lease. No proof? Someone else can sign a residence declaration on the form instead.
  • A NAATI-accredited translation, if it is not in English Or a translation from the licence country’s consulate in Australia.

What you do

Recognised country

  • Most drivers with a current full licence exchange it directly, no tests
  • A knowledge test may still apply if your licence expired more than 5 years ago

Experienced Driver Recognition country, aged 25+

  • These countries meet some, not all, of Australia’s testing standards
  • At 25 or over, your age and driving experience substitute for the tests
  • Under 25 with this same licence? Both the theory and the practical test are required

Any other country

  • Pass the driving theory test
  • Pass the practical driving test
  • Your class is then set by your age and years of driving experience, as if you had started in Australia

You must convert within 6 months of starting to live in Victoria — the clock starts the day you first begin living there, even if you leave and come back. Staying under 6 months? You can drive on your valid overseas licence, with a translation if needed.

How long

An eyesight test either way Then a single appointment if you are exempt from further testing — longer if you need to sit tests first.

What arrives

A Victorian driver licence In the class your experience and testing outcome puts you in.
Queensland A transport and motoring customer service centre
Queensland residents

What you need

  • Your original overseas licence Photocopies and certified copies are not accepted — bring the original.
  • Original evidence of identity Including proof that you live in Queensland.
  • A NAATI-approved translation, if it is not in English From an accredited National Accreditation Authority for Translators and Interpreters translator.

What you do

New Zealand or a recognised country

  • Held (or held within the last 5 years) a licence equivalent to class C, RE or R
  • No road rules test and no practical driving test needed
  • Recognised countries include the UK, USA, Canada, Singapore, Japan and others — check the current list before you go

Non-recognised country

  • Pass the road rules test first (written, or the PrepL online course)
  • Then book and pass the practical driving test — your overseas licence is valid to drive on until then
  • Fail the driving test and your authority to drive on the overseas licence is withdrawn immediately

The 3-month deadline is counted from when you became a citizen or were granted a resident visa while already living in Queensland — not from the day you arrived. Drivers 75 and over also need a medical certificate.

How long

One visit, if exempt Non-recognised applicants need the road rules test passed before a practical test can even be booked.

What arrives

A Queensland driver licence Same class as your overseas licence. Your authority to drive on that overseas licence is formally withdrawn the moment the Queensland licence is issued.
Do not let your entitlement to drive lapse

Driving on an overseas licence after the window your state allows has closed is a driving offence, and it can also void your car insurance if you are in a crash while unlicensed. If you are not sure your dates are still current, check with your state authority before you drive — not after.
