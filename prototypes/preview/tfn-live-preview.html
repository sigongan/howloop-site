How to apply for an Australian Tax File Number (TFN)

The right way to apply for a TFN depends entirely on who you are — a citizen or resident who can set up a Strong myID, a visa holder with work rights, a resident without a Digital ID, or someone living overseas with Australian income. See every route at once and find the one that fits.

Researched, illustrated and safety-checked by the Howloop editorial team · Published 12 August 2026 · Last reviewed 12 August 2026 · How we make guides

! This is a process guide, not tax advice

This page walks through how to apply for a TFN and which route fits your situation — it is not advice on whether you need one, how you should be taxed, or anything about a tax return. Howloop is not a registered tax agent. For anything specific to your circumstances, use the ATO directly or speak to a registered tax agent.

There are four completely different ways to apply for a Tax File Number. Here they all are, side by side — pick who you are and the rest fade out, or read all four.

Work out your route

Which TFN application is yours?

Tap who you are and the other lanes fade out — but you can always see the whole picture, including the ones that aren't yours.

Who are you?

myID + myGov Online, on your phone
Citizens & residents, 15+

What you need

  • Current Australian passport Your own, not a family member’s.
  • One more ID Driver's licence or learner's permit…
  • …or Medicare card Any one of these works.
  • …or citizenship certificate Any one of these works.

What you do

  • Set up the myID app — scan your passport, add one more ID, take a selfie
  • Sign in to myGov and link the ATO
  • Fill in the short TFN application

Residents can apply at any age — being at least 15 is only a requirement for this online myID route.

How long

Usually the same day On screen, straight away. If it needs checking, up to 28 days.

What arrives

Shows on screen A letter also lands in your myGov Inbox.
Individual Auto-Registration (IAR) Online, nothing to send
Visa with work rights

What you need

  • Nothing to post or photocopy Just your passport and visa details, typed in.

What you do

  • Check your visa shows as in effect on VEVO first
  • Open the ATO's online application for foreign passport holders
  • Enter your passport, visa and an Australian postal address

Blocked by visa conditions 8101, 8103 or 8116 — those mean no work, so this route won’t accept the application. Use the in-person route instead.

How long

Up to 28 days Applying a second time while you wait makes it slower, not faster.

What arrives

Letter in the post To the Australian address you gave.
Australian resident without a Digital ID Someone checks your ID in person, or by certified post
Residents, any age

What you need

  • Current original ID documents For the two in-person channels — not photocopies.
  • Certified copies For the postal channel only — never send originals.

What you do

Australia Post

  • Complete the online form and print the summary
  • Take it to a participating outlet within 30 days, with your original documents
  • Sign it there

Services Australia service centre

  • Complete the paper form NAT 1432
  • Bring current original documents to a service centre

By post

  • For people who can’t use either channel above
  • Complete NAT 1432 and send certified copies — never the originals — to the address on the form

Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander applicants who can’t provide enough identity documents can use form NAT 1589 instead — a referee completes the proof-of-identity reference.

How long

Up to 28 days Counted from when your documents were sighted, or received by post.

What arrives

Letter in the post Say on the form if you want it shared with Centrelink.
Paper form NAT 2628 Printed, posted, slowest
Living overseas

What you need

  • Form NAT 2628 Download from ato.gov.au, or order it on 1300 720 092.
  • Certified copies of your ID Copies, never the originals.
  • Postage To the address printed on the form.

What you do

  • Check you actually need one — interest, dividends or royalties alone usually don't require a TFN
  • Download and complete NAT 2628
  • Get your ID documents certified, then post everything together

How long

Up to 28 days after it arrives Plus international post time in both directions.

What arrives

Letter in the post To the address on your form.
Once you have it, guard it

Only your employer (after you start work), your bank, your super fund and a registered tax agent ever need your TFN. Never put it on a resume. The ATO will never text or email you a link asking you to log in, and will never ask for payment by gift card.
