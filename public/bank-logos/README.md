# Bank logo assets

Official artwork used to identify banking providers. Trademarks remain the property of their respective owners; their display does not imply endorsement or affiliation.

Source URLs and retrieval dates are recorded per provider in `src/data/bank-logos.json`. Sources are the logo URLs supplied by providers to the Australian CDR register or the provider's official website. Images are served locally, preserving their original colours and proportions. White/reversed artwork uses a dark backdrop.

The mapping deliberately stays separate from product snapshots so refreshing product data does not overwrite verified assets. To update a logo, retrieve the official image, replace its asset, and update the source and date in the mapping. Do not create imitation brand marks. Providers without a verified available asset use a neutral bank icon.
