## Why Howloop exists

Every professional kitchen runs on rules. Rest the steak. Salt the pasta water like the sea. Don't put potatoes in the fridge. Some of those rules are brilliant, some are half-right, and a few are simply wrong — but they are usually passed on without the *why*.

Howloop is where I chase the why. Each guide takes one everyday cooking question, explains what is happening at the level of proteins, starches, enzymes and heat, and then turns it back into something you can use at the stove. The name is the idea: you ask how, you loop back through the science, and you come out cooking better.

## Who writes it

I'm **Jess Baek**, a professional chef based in Far North Queensland, Australia.

I started as an apprentice in a luxury hotel kitchen in Seoul, worked as a demi chef in a Japanese izakaya in Vancouver, and have spent more than a decade in Australian kitchens since — as a sous chef, a senior chef de partie in a resort kitchen, and for five years as sous chef and then head chef of a one-hat Italian restaurant. My training spans Italian and Japanese cooking, and I still cook for a living every week.

Before I cooked, I studied mechanical engineering and worked as an assistant engineer on power-plant projects in Korea. That background is why Howloop cares about temperatures, pressures and measurements: a kitchen is a small factory full of heat transfer and chemistry, and understanding the system makes the results repeatable.

## How the guides are made

Every article starts from a real cooking question — a kitchen rule worth checking, a common mistake, or a topic from my food-science video series. Howloop uses AI tools to help with research and drafting. Every factual claim is then checked against the sources listed at the end of the article, with a strong preference for government food-safety agencies, peer-reviewed research, university extension services and standard references such as Harold McGee's *On Food and Cooking*.

Where professional kitchen habit and official food-safety guidance disagree, I say so plainly and give you the official guidance. You can read the full details in our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/).
