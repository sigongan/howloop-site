## Email

The best way to reach Howloop is by email: **[hello@howloop.how](mailto:hello@howloop.how)**.

Messages are read by Jess Baek, who writes the site.

## What to write about

- **Corrections.** If you think something in an article is wrong or out of date, please tell us which page and what you believe is incorrect, ideally with a source. Confirmed errors are fixed and the article's "updated" date is changed.
- **Questions and topic ideas.** If there is a cooking question you would like explained — why something happens, or whether a kitchen rule actually holds up — send it in. Many Howloop articles start this way.
- **Collaborations and media.** For brand, media or video enquiries, include a short description of what you have in mind.

## What we can't help with

Howloop publishes general information. We can't give personal medical, dietary or allergy advice, assess whether a specific food you have is safe to eat, or provide consulting for food businesses by email. For those, please speak to a qualified health professional or your local food-safety authority.

## Privacy

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it only to reply and to improve the site. See the [privacy policy](/privacy/) for details.
