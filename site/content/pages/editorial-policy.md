## Who is responsible

Howloop is written and published by [Jess Baek](/about/), a professional chef in Queensland, Australia. Jess chooses every topic and is responsible for everything published on the site.

## How topics are chosen

We write about questions that come up in real cooking: kitchen rules that are repeated without explanation, common mistakes and their causes, and food myths that deserve a closer look. We favour evergreen topics — things that will still be useful in five years — over news.

## How articles are researched and written

1. **Start with the question.** Each article answers one clear question and is written for people who cook, not for search engines.
2. **Go to good sources.** We prefer government food-safety agencies (such as Food Standards Australia New Zealand and the US Department of Agriculture), peer-reviewed research, university extension services and standard reference books such as Harold McGee's *On Food and Cooking*. Popular articles and videos are used only as pointers to better evidence.
3. **Use AI as a tool, openly.** Howloop uses AI tools to help research topics, organise information and draft text. AI output is treated as a draft, not a source: factual claims are checked against the references listed at the end of each article, and anything we cannot support is removed.
4. **Show the evidence.** Every article ends with its sources so you can check our work. Where evidence is limited or experts disagree, we say so.
5. **Add kitchen context.** Where it helps, articles explain how professional kitchens handle the same problem. We describe common restaurant practice; we do not invent personal stories or test results.

## Food safety

When we mention internal temperatures, storage or raw ingredients, we give the relevant official guidance. If restaurant practice differs — for example, cooking a steak below the temperature consumer guidelines recommend — we point out the difference rather than present the restaurant practice as risk-free. People at higher risk from foodborne illness should follow official guidance.

## Corrections and updates

If you think we got something wrong, please [contact us](/contact/). We review every correction request. When we fix a factual error or materially update an article, we change its "updated" date.

## Independence and advertising

Howloop is supported by display advertising served by Google AdSense. Advertisers have no influence over what we write, and ads are not endorsements. We do not publish sponsored articles or accept payment for coverage. If that ever changes, the content will be clearly labelled.
