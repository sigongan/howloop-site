This policy explains how **howloop.how** ("Howloop", "we", "us") handles information when you visit the site or contact us. Howloop is run by Jess Baek in Queensland, Australia.

## Information we collect

**When you browse.** Howloop has no user accounts, comments, sign-up forms or analytics scripts of its own. We do not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details to read the site.

**When you email us.** If you write to [hello@howloop.how](mailto:hello@howloop.how), we receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use these only to reply, to fix errors you report and to improve the site, and we do not sell or share them.

## Hosting

The site is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare necessarily processes technical information about each request — such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested — to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. See [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) for how it handles this information.

## Advertising and cookies

Howloop uses **Google AdSense** to show advertising, which helps pay for the site. Google and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, device identifiers and similar technologies to serve and measure ads.

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
- You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's [My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/) or [Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).
- You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at [www.aboutads.info/choices](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/) (United States) or [www.youronlinechoices.eu](https://www.youronlinechoices.eu/) (Europe).

If you opt out, you may still see ads, but they will be less tailored to you — for example, based on the page you are reading rather than your browsing history.

To learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

**Visitors in the UK, European Economic Area and Switzerland.** Where the law requires it, a consent message managed through Google will ask for your permission before cookies are used for personalised advertising. You can change your choice at any time using the privacy link that appears with that message.

**Managing cookies in your browser.** Most browsers let you block or delete cookies. Blocking all cookies may affect how some websites work.

## Children

Howloop is written for a general adult audience of people who cook. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Links to other sites

Articles link to sources such as government agencies, universities and scientific journals. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

## Your choices and rights

You can ask what personal information we hold about you (in practice, only emails you have sent us), ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it by emailing [hello@howloop.how](mailto:hello@howloop.how). We aim to handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles and other privacy laws that apply to us.

## Changes to this policy

We will update this page if our practices change — for example, if we add analytics — and change the "last updated" date above.
