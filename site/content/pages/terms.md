## General information only

Howloop explains food science and cooking techniques for a general audience. It is not medical, nutritional, allergy or professional food-safety advice for your particular circumstances. Food safety depends on details we cannot see — how food was stored, the equipment you use, who will eat it — so always follow the guidance of your local food-safety authority, and take extra care when cooking for young children, older people, pregnant people or anyone with a weakened immune system.

Where an article mentions temperatures, times or techniques used in restaurant kitchens, it says so. Those practices can differ from official consumer guidance, and the official guidance should take priority at home.
